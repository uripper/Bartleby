## About

Bartleby is a desktop based **reading schedule optimizer** that generates a daily reading plan based on your book backlog. Let it know what you want to read, how fast you read, how much time you have, and it will create a schedule that fits you.

## Why?

Bartleby was mainly built for myself. I wanted to visually see just how many books a year I could read with a small time commitment each day. I'm a fairly slow reader, and I have severe ADHD, which makes it pretty hard to believe that I can make meaningful progress on my backlog. And having a giant backlog of books doesn't feel as life affirming to me as it is for [Umberto Eco and Nassim Nicholas Taleb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antilibrary). Ultimately, this became a fun way to get myself to read more. I hope that others find it useful too.

## What it Does

- Generates daily reading sessions from To Be Read books based on various preferences
- Replans sessions intelligently on command, preserving anything you've already completed
- Tracks your progress with books, estimates when you'll finish a book, and maintains a history of your read books
- Gives stats about predicted number of book finishes, minutes read, and more
- Let's you customize how it schedules and when it schedules

## What it Uses

Bartleby is built as a Tauri application with a Rust Backend and a TypeScript Frontend.

## Current Status

Bartleby is currently available in an early alpha build for Windows and Mac OS. Currently you can:

- Add books
- Create a reading schedule
- View your reading schedule for the day
- Log your reading progress
- View your reading history and stats
- Adjust many customizable parameters for how the reading schedule is generated

Numerous bugs are present and most if not all features may not be supported with later versions.
